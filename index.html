Get your export

  1. In Meta Accounts Center, choose your Instagram profile and request an export to your device.
  2. Select Followers and following. Choose All time and either JSON or HTML.
  3. Download the ZIP when it’s ready and open it here.

Your files stay on this device. The comparison runs in your browser.
