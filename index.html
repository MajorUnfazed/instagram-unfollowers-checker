How to get your Instagram data:

  1. Open Instagram app or website
  2. Go to Settings → Your Activity → Download Your Information
  3. Request a download (JSON format)
  4. Wait for Instagram to prepare your data (can take a few days)
  5. Download and extract the zip file
  6. Find followers_1.json and following.json in the connections/followers_and_following folder

⚠️ Your data is processed locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
